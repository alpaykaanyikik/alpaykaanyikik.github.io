CardKeep / KartKasa – Support

Need help with CardKeep? A few tips first:

  • The app works fully offline – no internet needed.
  • To move a purchase to a new device, tap Settings → Restore Purchases on the new device.
  • To back up your data, use Settings → Export data (vCard or CSV).

If you still need help, contact the developer at the email listed on the app's store page.
