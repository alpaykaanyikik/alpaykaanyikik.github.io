CardKeep / KartKasa – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-19

CardKeep (Turkish: KartKasa) is a 100% offline business card scanner. This policy explains what data the app processes and where it goes.

1. What data we collect

None. CardKeep does not collect, transmit, store on any server, or share any personal or technical data. There is no account, no login, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no advertising SDK.

2. What stays on your device

All information you enter or scan – card images, contact fields (name, title, company, phone, email, address, website, notes), projects/folders – is stored locally on your device only, inside the app's private storage. Nothing is uploaded.

3. Permissions

  • Camera – to photograph business cards. Images are processed on-device only.
  • Photos / Media – so you can import an existing card image from your gallery.
  • Contacts – only used if you tap "Add to Contacts" to save a scanned card to your phone's address book.

The app makes no network calls. The Android INTERNET permission may appear in the manifest solely because Google Play Billing requires it for in-app purchases; the app code itself never contacts any external server.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases (Pro and Lifetime unlocks) are processed by the Google Play Store / Apple App Store. We do not receive your payment details. The purchase state is stored locally on your device and can be restored via the Restore button.

5. Children

CardKeep is a general productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13.

6. Data export and deletion

You can export all your cards as vCard (.vcf) or CSV from Settings, and you can delete all data from the app at any time from Settings → Delete all data. Uninstalling the app removes all stored data as well.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL.

8. Contact

Questions or requests: please contact the developer at the email listed on the app's store page.
